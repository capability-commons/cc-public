H. How this was made, and what it does not claim

  1. The capture. One public {{ d.observation.source_kind }}, attributed as it attributes itself, preserved unchanged with its locator, times and digest, imported by a deterministic command. No model took part.
  2. The need. A model wrote what the post supports, stated without a product, with evidence that says what one post does and does not establish; five need evals judged it as it was written, redrafting on an unmet verdict within a budget.
  3. The concepts. The same need was seen through {{ d.concept|map(attribute='framing.id')|unique|list|length }} framings; under each a model proposed a candidate solution with its assumptions and their evidence, its risks and its candidate requirements, and a second model attacked it for up to three rounds; the last challenge and the resolution before it are in section C.
  4. Promotion. Each concept's candidate requirements became proposed requirement items by a function, deriving from the concept and the need, and the eleven requirement evals judged them: {{ d.finding_count }} findings, in section D.
  5. Assessment. Each concept's feasibility was assessed, by an agent with tools that captured its references, or by a model without references; each assessment says which, and the briefing leads with the referenced one where it exists.

Standing

Every item in these documents is proposed. The trace reports, for each proposed requirement, that nothing implements it and nothing verifies it. Acceptance is refused until derivation, code, verification and current evidence are all present.

What these documents do not claim

The post is one self-reported event: it establishes neither frequency nor prevalence, and its attribution is as observed, not a verified identity or an authority to speak. A concept is a hypothesis under a lens, not a design. A requirement here is a proposal, not an obligation anyone has accepted. A product a model names without a reference is the model's knowledge, not a finding. The fixed shape, one need and one concept per framing, is the demonstration's choice, not a method.
