Standing

Every item above is proposed. The trace reports, for each proposed requirement, that nothing implements it and nothing verifies it. Acceptance is refused until derivation, code, verification and current evidence are all present; the tree already holds the machinery for that half.

What this briefing does not claim

The post is one self-reported event: it establishes neither frequency nor prevalence, and its attribution is as observed, not a verified identity or an authority to speak. A concept is a hypothesis under a lens, not a design. A requirement here is a proposal, not an obligation anyone has accepted. The fixed shape, one need and one concept per framing, is the demonstration's choice, not a method.
